# Table Serve

**Let your customers order from their phone — no app, no waiter needed.**

Table Serve turns any restaurant table into a smart ordering point. You stick a small NFC sticker on the table, your customer taps it with their phone, your full menu appears instantly, and they order right there. The order goes straight to your kitchen. No app download. No account creation. No waiting for a waiter to come take the order.

---

## What problem does this solve?

Every restaurant faces the same pain points:

- Customers wait too long to get someone's attention to order
- Staff run back and forth taking orders, reading handwriting, and repeating items to the kitchen
- Mistakes happen — wrong items, missed instructions, forgotten allergies
- During busy hours, tables go unserved for too long

Table Serve removes the middleman for the ordering step. Your staff can focus on cooking and delivering food rather than taking orders.

## How it works — in plain English

### Step 1: You set up your menu (takes about 30 minutes)

You log in to your private dashboard and add:
- Your menu items (name, price, description, photo)
- Categories (Starters, Mains, Desserts, Drinks — whatever you like)
- Special tags — mark items as Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free, Chef's Special
- Spice levels, allergen warnings, calories — all optional

### Step 2: You add your tables

You add each table in your restaurant to the system (Table 1, Table 2, Outdoor Patio, etc.). The system generates a unique link for each table automatically.

### Step 3: You get NFC stickers for your tables

You buy cheap NFC stickers online (they cost pennies each). Using a free app on your phone, you write the table's unique link onto the sticker and stick it on the table. That's it.

### Step 4: Customers tap and order

A customer sits down, taps the sticker with their phone — your branded menu opens in their browser. They browse, add items to their cart, and place the order. They can add notes like "no onions" or "extra sauce." Your menu, your colors, your logo — it looks like your restaurant's own app.

### Step 5: The order arrives at your screen

The moment the customer places the order, it appears on your orders screen. You can see exactly which table it came from, what they ordered, any special instructions, and the total amount. You tap to confirm it, update it as it's being prepared, and mark it as ready when the food is done.

The customer's phone shows them a live status — "Order received", "Being prepared", "Your food is ready."

The company that runs Table Serve has a separate management area. They:

- Handle subscription plans
- Cannot see your menu or orders — your data is yours

They never create an account. They never log in. They just tap and order.

## Common questions

**Do my customers need to download an app?**
No. The menu opens directly in their phone's browser — Safari, Chrome, whatever they normally use.

**What phones does it work on?**
Any iPhone from iPhone 7 onwards and most Android phones from the last 5 years support NFC tap.

**What if a customer doesn't have NFC?**
You can also print a QR code for the same link and put it on the table. Customers can scan it with their camera app instead.

**Can I change my menu while the restaurant is open?**
Yes. Any change you make in the dashboard appears on the customer's menu immediately.

**Can customers order from outside the restaurant?**
The system is designed to require a scan of the physical table sticker. Without tapping the sticker in person, the link doesn't grant access to place an order. This is intentional — so random people online can't send orders to your kitchen.

**What happens if I run out of a dish?**
Go to your menu, find the item, and toggle it off. It disappears from the customer menu instantly. Toggle it back on when it's available again.

**Do I need internet at the restaurant?**
Yes. The system runs online. Your customers need a phone signal or Wi-Fi connection. Standard restaurant Wi-Fi is fine.

**Is my data secure?**
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit. Your menu, orders, and customer information are stored securely and are not shared with anyone.
